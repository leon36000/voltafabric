# R429 — Resident SC117 CPU output head

Parent R428 `1436b889b821884abf66092f93f1c290aa54c651`. Implements a CPU-only resident output head for this observed SC117 checkpoint: four-stream output hyperconnection mix and a chunked Q6_K projection over all 248,320 vocabulary rows, with deterministic lowest-index argmax on ties. It retains the 521,472,000 bytes of projection weights as 31 resident native GGML matrices of at most 8,192 rows, plus the three BF16/F32 hyperconnection tensors. The manifest binds every span to a SHA-256 and the loader validates bounded contiguous chunks, vocabulary coverage, source sizes and metadata. The API accepts a supplied run/model/layer/epoch identity and complete residual but does not prove the residual was produced by the final layer or bind it to a reserved transaction. The caller must establish final-layer provenance and generation policy.

Architecture is adapted from Strata `fb58e0dbc8399662c0e47c76578c6e878b14f6cf` (MIT), `src/core/layer.cpp` `lm_head_mix` and `lm_head`, plus the VoltaFabric CPU hyperconnection already traced in R404.
