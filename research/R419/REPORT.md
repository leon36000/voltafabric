# R419 — prepare/commit GDN candidate for enclosing-layer transactions

The resident SC117 GDN prefix now offers `prepare` instead of immediately committing the prefix. It checks run/model/layer/epoch against the stage, requires a nonempty plan and a nonzero attempt, and binds the complete run/model/plan/layer/batch/epoch `Stamp` and attempt to the candidate. It also checks the process-local token index. It computes with a cloned GDN state, producing an immutable output view and a private candidate. Each stage instance has a unique process-local ID. Dropping a proposal leaves the resident state unchanged. `commit_validated` accepts the exact same stamp and attempt, the same stage and current token, and a finite complete-shaped 10240-element residual before swapping in the candidate and advancing the token. Old candidates, altered batch/plan/attempt, wrong stage and malformed residual are rejected without changing the stage.

This is not yet the enclosing-layer transaction: `commit_validated` trusts the caller to have validated complete routed/shared outputs, plan/registry, durable reservation and the origin of the final residual. Shape and finiteness do not prove actual completeness. The prefix does not own the residual between calls and does not persist state or protect against a crash after submission. A future orchestrator must bind it to the validated Collector and shared expert, atomically own the residual, and enforce durable no-replay for UNKNOWN. It must not treat this helper alone as production qualification.
