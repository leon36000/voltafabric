# R510 — local real-worker exercise of alternate-rank eviction plan

Parent GitLab R509 a8f9f7a85ee188700470d5beef034e6cd25d34a2. An explicit, ignored-by-default test exercises R508's in-memory rank alternatives with two actual local CPU `vf-rank-session` child processes and five synthetic zero-valued GGML experts. Rank 3 owns three experts all selected by the committed batch; rank 2 owns two, only one selected. Under synthetic per-rank payload caps, the larger rank 3 cannot satisfy the conservative reservation without removing protected experts, but rank 2 can after removing its unselected expert.

The test checks that choosing the complete plan sends no worker traffic, chooses rank 2, reserves a unique eviction, obtains the actual rank-2 worker acknowledgement while rank 3 receives no eviction request, refuses duplicate reservation, updates the test routing registry and order, then executes a second batch on both workers and verifies their clean close counts. This manually connects the planner to the real session eviction protocol; it does not exercise the full Segment/source integration, a real model, nonzero numerical outputs, SSH, GPU or an inter-PC lease. Caps in this test are synthetic payload accounting, not hardware resource admission.

The corrected test passed with debug and release test binaries; targeted Clippy with tests passed. The first fresh attempt failed after the acknowledged eviction because its test routing fixture still listed the removed expert as owned. The fixture was corrected before the next fresh attempts; this was not a retried operation. Its three claims and the six claims from the two successful attempts are preserved byte-for-byte in CLAIMS.json, all RESERVED_UNKNOWN_NO_REPLAY. The independent verifier checks their raw SHA256, distinct run/batch identities and operation filenames. The mere presence of a reservation is not an execution receipt.
