# R476 — Experimental per-rank expert payload budgets

Parent: R475, GitLab commit 42c7f70a16bf4b9e6b9b069cb417a79ff956ed5b. This branch is experimental and is not a qualification of the new limits for generation.

The existing expert source gains optional per-rank payload limits, deterministic placement by relative payload use, and accounting on admission and acknowledged eviction. A rank limit must be positive, within the global limit, and supplied for every rank. The prefill program accepts VF_RANK2_BUDGET and VF_RANK3_BUDGET only together and records per-position totals when it succeeds. Without these variables, placement remains the legacy selection. These are payload counters, not measurements of physical host memory, network placement, leases, or topology. Per-rank eviction is not implemented; the existing headroom eviction is global and may not relieve the needed rank.

Four source tests passed in debug and release; Clippy passed for the source and prefill binary with warnings denied. They exercise limit validation, placement, checked arithmetic and refusal of unacknowledged or repeated release.

A fresh bounded local CPU run on PC1 used 48 layers, 96 workers, prompt Hello!, at most eight predictions, a 3 GiB global limit, 1 GiB rank 2 and 2 GiB rank 3. Both ranks were local labels on PC1. The runner refused the run and published no result (zero-byte result file). Its wrapper discarded the engine stderr and therefore the immediate cause is unknown; the recorded claims do not prove the positions completed or identify which capacity or other condition caused the refusal. There were no rank workers remaining after refusal. The 335 ledger claims, all RESERVED_UNKNOWN_NO_REPLAY, are preserved in claims/ and checked by verify.py. They include 240 layer claims over batches 0–4 and 95 eviction claims; their existence is not proof of successful operations. Do not replay them.
