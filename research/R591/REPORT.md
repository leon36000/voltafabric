# R591 — broaden the cross-build scalar witness

Using the R590 executable and observed SC117 layer-2 expert slices 12, 75 and 255, a new CPU-only experiment compared PC1 x86_64 and PC6 AArch64 on sixteen finite FP32 activation vectors. Two are the retained R428 position-0 and position-1 activations; the remaining fourteen are deterministic scaled variants, not independent model tokens. Each of the 48 expert/input pairs produced exactly identical 31,760-byte captures on the two hosts, covering Q8_K input, 640 scalar gate values, 640 scalar up values, 640 nonlinear intermediates, Q8_0 intermediate and 2,560 scalar f64 down outputs. All outputs were finite and nonzero. The three first-position unscaled captures also match the earlier R590 records exactly.

The layer's gate/up tensor is IQ2_S and down tensor IQ4_NL. GGML decoders/quantizers are shared, and `std::exp`, compiler, math library and GGML binaries differ across hosts; exact agreement here is an observation, not a general determinism guarantee. This is one layer, two original related activations, their scaled variants and three experts. It does not qualify other formats, arbitrary inputs, GPU arithmetic, model quality, an independent full-model oracle or distributed execution; nor does it change the original R428 vector/scalar failure. The captured scalars are a candidate reference, not integrated production code.

`make_inputs.py` reproduces the variants from hash-checked retained R428 inputs; model-derived inputs and raw captures remain local. The externally prepared PLAN binds the runner, input manifest, source and binaries before either run. Each host ran 48 standalone diagnostics in a CPU scope capped at 512 MiB, zero swap, 50% CPU and 16 tasks. Separate exclusive submitted markers and output creation prevent accidental overwrite; no worker, GPU, R53 reservation or UNKNOWN was replayed. `verify.py` independently parses all capture structures and finite values, checks every host pair and earlier R590 baselines, and retains hashes in RESULT.json. R53, the real shared lease and heterogeneous production compatibility remain open.
