# R428 — Persistent CPU owners for two distinct layer inputs

Parent R427 `00badc035a585c871142a18fb61fda269e333d7e`. Corrected the bound dispatch to derive its complete expert set from the supplied resident registry, not just the selected route. The previous implementation could reject a worker stack containing unselected but resident experts; a native worker requires its full physical order on every request. The change keeps all resident owners in the routing metadata while the selected route and weights still come only from the bound layer. Registry and physical-order validation remain active.

One R428 local CPU run used two distinct synthetic perturbations of a model-derived layer-2 residual (input SHA-256s `ed1d74a7f93128f6b6a3b30855687a660cba93eca8e1c76c72086dc942fa7061` and `9a69b12aec635edd0a042269e830779b49be0db018f6812479f617867371d96d`). They represent two inputs to the same layer, not generated language-model tokens and not a dependency between these two residuals. The same GDN, post-attention and shared stages and the same two workers stayed resident. Twelve owners, six per worker, covered the union of the two different top-10 routes. Both workers reported exactly two executions and exited normally; GDN advanced from zero to two, with two distinct batch/attempt claims (`CLAIMS.json`). Both claims remain `RESERVED_UNKNOWN_NO_REPLAY` and must not be replayed. The 12 native expert slices matched the selected GGUF slices (`OBSERVATION.json`, a local observation rather than checkpoint authentication).

The live GDN output hashes exactly matched an independently executed two-step preview; its synthetic collector exists only to advance the GDN candidate in the preview. A separate NumPy f64 recurrence oracle using the observed QKV projections found max absolute differences 4.20e-9 and 3.76e-9 for tokens 0 and 1. For token 1, the nonzero prior-state contribution differed from a zero-state computation by up to 0.00646, demonstrating that the second step exercised history and decay. This oracle consumes QKV instead of independently validating all projection rows. The actual layer's routes and weights matched the separate post-attention preview exactly.
