# R560 — QSA batch eviction refusal and lost acknowledgement

Two fresh local CPU segments ran the same two real layer-3 positions and produced the R557 residual hashes. For the idle-rank case, both rank-2 residents were unused and all routed experts were on rank 3. Attempting to evict a rank-2 resident was refused before a new claim or network frame; accounting and the committed position remained unchanged, and both sessions closed cleanly.

For the lost-ack case, both ranks participated and 17 experts were admitted dynamically. The diagnostic proxy, adapted from the already qualified R465 proxy, allowed rank 2 to execute an eviction of expert 0, persisted the acknowledgement identity and digest, and intentionally suppressed that reply. The coordinator observed SESSION_CLOSED, kept the expert charged and registered, marked the segment terminal and refused the next batch without creating a claim. Rank 3 closed cleanly and the isolated proxy reaped its child. The proxy marker's complete stamp, attempt, binding and operation ID match the separately persisted eviction claim. This deliberately leaves the physical eviction outcome unknown to the coordinator: the claim is never replayed.

All three fresh local claims remain RESERVED_UNKNOWN_NO_REPLAY. The independent `verify.py` checks the stored marker, claims and outcomes. Clippy passed for the native-feature library and diagnostic with warnings denied and the existing trace-only dead-code warning allowed; Rustfmt passed. No GPU, inter-PC transport, recovery after UNKNOWN, or model-wide prefill is qualified.
